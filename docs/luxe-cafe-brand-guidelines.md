# Luxe Café — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Luxe Café is a premium coffee and pastry brand offering a modern, sophisticated café experience. The identity balances warmth and refinement — approachable yet elevated.

---

## Logo System

### Primary Logo
Cup icon + "Luxe Café" wordmark with "Premium Coffee & Pastry" descriptor. Use on storefront, packaging, menus, and digital presence.

### Stacked
Cup icon centered above wordmark. Use on square formats, social avatars, and merchandise.

### Cup Mark
The abstract espresso cup alone. Use as watermark, favicon, and decorative element.

### Minimum Sizes
- Primary: 80px wide (digital), 1 in (print)
- Cup mark: 20px (digital)

---

## Color Palette

| Swatch | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Espresso | #3C2415 | Primary brand color, backgrounds, packaging |
| Caramel | #C68E4E | Secondary accent, CTAs, highlights |
| Charcoal | #2C1810 | Text, dark mode |
| Warm Taupe | #A0846A | Secondary text, muted elements |
| Cream | #FFF8F0 | Background base — warm, inviting |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards, surfaces |

Dark espresso anchors the brand. Caramel provides warmth and approachability. Cream keeps the palette light and premium.

---

## Typography

### Display: Playfair Display
Used for: logo, headlines, menu headers, pull quotes.
- Weight range: 400–700
- Elegant serif with refined contrast — feels premium and timeless

### Body: Inter
Used for: menu items, descriptions, labels, web copy.
- Weight range: 300–600
- Clean sans-serif for readability at small sizes

### Hierarchy
- Logo: 44px / 700 / -0.5px tracking
- H1: 56–80px / 700 / -2px
- H2: 28–44px / 600 / -0.5 to -1.5px
- Body: 14–18px / 300–400
- Caption: 10–12px / 500–600 / +0.02em uppercase

---

## Brand Voice

- Warm and welcoming
- Refined without being pretentious
- Passionate about craft and quality
- Speaks to coffee lovers and casual guests alike

---

## Usage Rules

- Lead with dark espresso; use cream as the primary background
- Caramel is the accent — use for CTAs, highlights, and decorative elements
- Photography should be warm-toned — natural light, coffee shots, cozy interiors
- The cup icon can be used as a repeating pattern element
- Maintain generous whitespace — the brand breathes
- Avoid harsh neons, cool blues, or sterile whites
