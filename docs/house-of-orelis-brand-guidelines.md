# House of Orelis — Brand Usage Guidelines

## Brand Overview

House of Orelis is a luxury fashion and accessories brand targeting high-end customers. The visual identity is built on elegance, sophistication, and timeless refinement.

---

## Logo System

### Primary Logo
Full lockup: icon mark + "ORELIS" wordmark. Use on all primary brand communications. Clear space equal to the height of the icon mark on all sides.

### Secondary Logo
Stacked or horizontal variant with "Luxury Fashion" descriptor. Use when additional context is needed or in space-constrained layouts.

### Icon Mark
The geometric diamond mark alone. Use as a watermark, avatar, favicon, or decorative element. Never rotate, distort, or recolor outside the approved palette.

### Minimum Sizes
- Primary logo: 120px wide (digital), 1.5 in (print)
- Icon mark: 24px (digital), 0.3 in (print)

---

## Color Palette

| Swatch | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Burgundy | #6B1A2A | Primary brand color, backgrounds, accents |
| Gold | #C9A84C | Secondary accent, highlights, CTAs |
| Charcoal | #1A1A1A | Primary text, dark backgrounds |
| Taupe | #8A7A6A | Secondary text, muted elements |
| Ivory | #FAF5F0 | Background, light mode canvas |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards, surfaces |

Use burgundy as the primary color. Gold is an accent only — never overwhelming. Ivory provides warmth to otherwise neutral layouts.

---

## Typography

### Display: Cormorant Garamond
Used for: headlines, logo wordmark, pull quotes, collection names.
- Weight range: 300–700
- Letter-spacing: +0.02em to +0.08em depending on size

### Body: Inter
Used for: body copy, descriptions, labels, navigation.
- Weight range: 300–600
- Letter-spacing: +0.01em at small sizes

### Hierarchy
- Logo: 56px / 700 / +0.08em tracking
- H1: 48–72px / 600–700 / +0.04em
- H2: 32–40px / 600 / +0.02em
- Body: 14–18px / 300–400
- Caption/Label: 11–12px / 600 / +0.02em uppercase

---

## Brand Voice

- Refined, not loud
- Confident, not arrogant
- Warm, not cold
- Minimal, not sparse

Write in a tone that communicates exclusivity and craft. Short sentences. Intentional vocabulary.

---

## Usage Rules

- Never place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a clearspace buffer
- Always maintain the gold-to-burgundy ratio — gold is 10–20% max of any composition
- Use ivory or white as the primary background; burgundy for emphasis
- Photography should feel editorial — high contrast, dramatic lighting, neutral or warm tones
- Avoid trends. The identity should feel timeless, not fashionable
