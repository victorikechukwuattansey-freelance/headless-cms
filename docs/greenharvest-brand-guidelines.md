# Green Harvest Organics — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Green Harvest Organics is an eco-friendly organic food brand rooted in sustainable agriculture. The identity communicates natural goodness, environmental responsibility, and wholesome quality.

---

## Logo System

### Primary Horizontal
Leaf icon + "Green Harvest" wordmark (Fraunces) + "Organics" descriptor. Use on packaging, website, and marketing materials.

### Stacked
Leaf icon centered above the wordmark. Use on square formats, social media, and product badges.

### Leaf Mark
The abstract leaf icon alone. Use as watermark, favicon, and decorative pattern element.

### Minimum Sizes
- Primary: 100px wide (digital), 1.2 in (print)
- Leaf icon: 20px (digital)

---

## Color Palette

| Swatch | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | #2D5A27 | Primary brand color, packaging, headers |
| Terracotta | #C46745 | Secondary accent, CTAs, highlights |
| Sage | #8DB580 | Supporting green, illustrations, backgrounds |
| Warm Cream | #F5F0E8 | Background base, natural feel |
| Charcoal | #2D2A24 | Text, dark mode |
| Slate Green | #7A8A70 | Secondary text, captions |

Forest green is the primary color. Terracotta provides warmth as an accent. Sage and cream create the natural, organic foundation.

---

## Typography

### Display: Fraunces
Used for: headlines, logo, packaging hero text, pull quotes.
- Weight range: 400–700
- Soft, warm serif that feels artisanal and natural

### Body: Inter
Used for: body copy, ingredients, nutritional info, web copy.
- Weight range: 300–600
- Clean, legible sans-serif for readability

### Hierarchy
- Logo: 44px / 700 / -0.5px tracking
- H1: 56–72px / 700 / -2px
- H2: 28–36px / 600 / -0.5px
- Body: 14–18px / 400
- Caption: 10–12px / 500 / +0.02em uppercase

---

## Brand Voice

- Warm and inviting
- Honest and transparent
- Educational without being preachy
- Celebrates nature and craftsmanship

---

## Usage Rules

- Lead with forest green; use cream as the primary background
- The leaf mark can be used as a repeating pattern at low opacity
- Photography should be natural light, earth tones, farm-to-table style
- Badges (USDA Organic, Non-GMO, Fair Trade) are part of the visual system
- Maintain generous whitespace — the brand breathes
- Never use harsh neons, synthetic-looking gradients, or overly polished finishes
