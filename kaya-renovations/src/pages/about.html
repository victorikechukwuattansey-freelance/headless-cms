About us

A family business that treats your home like its own.

Our story

Kaya Renovations started in 2016 as a two-man tiling crew — Chief Adewale Kaya and his son Tunde — working out of the back of a pickup in Ikeja. We grew by keeping one promise: finish what you start, and finish it properly.

Today we're a 14-person team of builders, tilers, plumbers and painters, and we've completed more than 300 projects across Lagos. The promise hasn't changed. Every job still gets a fixed written quote, a written schedule, and a two-year workmanship warranty.

We're licensed by the Lagos State Ministry of Physical Planning and fully insured for public liability, so you're protected on every project.
