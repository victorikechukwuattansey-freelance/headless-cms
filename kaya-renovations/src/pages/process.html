How we work

Five steps. One price. No surprises.

A renovation is stressful when it's unpredictable. Ours isn't — here's exactly what happens from first call to handover.

  1. 1

    Free site visit

    We come to you, measure everything, check the structure, and listen to what you actually want. 45–60 minutes, completely free, no pressure. This is also when we'll flag anything you hadn't considered — damp, old wiring, tricky access — before it becomes your problem.

  2. 2

    Written quote within 3 days

    You receive an itemised quote by email or WhatsApp: materials, labour, timeline, payment schedule — every line accounted for. The price is fixed. The only way it changes is if you change the scope, and you'll approve any change in writing first.

  3. 3

    Plan & schedule

    Materials are ordered with receipts shared with you. Your project manager confirms the start date and the day-by-day schedule, and sets up the WhatsApp group where you'll get updates and can ask questions anytime.

  4. 4

    We build

    The site stays tidy: floors sheeted, dust contained, rubbish cleared daily. You get a progress photo every evening. A senior builder inspects the work weekly — and if we're running late through our own fault, you get 5% off the final bill, automatically.

  5. 5

    Handover & warranty

    We walk through the finished work together and fix any snags on the spot. You receive your 2-year workmanship warranty (5 years for waterproofing) in writing, plus a care guide for everything we installed.

What you get on every project

  • • A single point of contact — your project manager
  • • Itemised, fixed written quote
  • • Written schedule with committed dates
  • • Daily WhatsApp updates & weekly photos
  • • Receipts for all materials
  • • 2-year workmanship warranty in writing
