Frequently asked questions

Are your quotes really fixed?

Yes. The only thing that changes the price is a change to the agreed scope, and we always get your written approval before spending anything extra. Our "done-on-time, on-price" promise is part of every contract.

Do we need to move out during the work?

For most kitchens, bathrooms and repaints — no. We contain dust, protect floors and work around you. For full extensions and major structural work, we'll advise on which parts of the house need to be vacated and for how long, before you commit.

How is payment structured?

30% to start, 30% at the midpoint, 30% at completion, and 10% held for 30 days after handover while you check the work. For larger extensions we agree a phased payment plan linked to milestones.

Can we choose our own materials?

Absolutely — and many clients do. We'll advise on quality and compatibility, buy with you or from you, and give you the receipts. We never mark up materials you source yourself.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Lagos: Ikeja, Surulere, Lekki, Victoria Island, Yaba, Gbagada, Magodo and beyond. Projects outside Lagos are considered case by case.

How long does a typical kitchen renovation take?

3–4 weeks including design, demolition, services and finishing. You'll get a day-by-day schedule in writing before we start, and we report against it every week.
