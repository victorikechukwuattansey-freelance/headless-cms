Features

Everything you need to ship AI automation

One dashboard to design, run, and debug your AI workflows from trigger to output.

Visual workflow builder

Drag, connect, and configure nodes on a DAG canvas. Triggers, AI steps, routing, and integrations snap together in minutes.

Multi-model AI gateway

Call Ollama, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini from the same workflow. Swap providers per node with a single dropdown.

Live execution monitoring

Watch every run in real time — node-by-node status, latency, tokens, and outputs, with full run history and drill-down.

Queues & retries

Reliable delivery with job queues, automatic retries with backoff, and dead-letter handling — built in, not bolted on.

Team workspaces

Share workflows with your team. Roles, permissions, version history, and comments keep collaboration clean.

API & webhooks

Trigger workflows from anywhere with REST endpoints and webhooks. Connect to 100+ tools and your own stack.
